# Ethereum

## Overview
Ethereum is the dominant smart contract platform and layer-1 blockchain, serving as the foundation for most DeFi applications, NFTs, and Web3 infrastructure. It introduced programmable blockchain — not just transfers, but arbitrary logic.

## Key Facts
- **Launched:** July 30, 2015
- **Founder:** Vitalik Buterin
- **Ticker:** ETH
- **Market Cap:** $200-400B (varies)
- **Consensus:** Proof of Stake (since The Merge, Sep 2022 — moved from PoW)
- **Block time:** ~12 seconds
- **Current TPS:** ~15-30 (before Danksharding)
- **Gas costs:** Variable; major pain point during congestion

## The Merge (2022)
Ethereum shifted from Proof of Work to Proof of Stake, reducing energy consumption ~99.95%. Staking now secures the network with ~25M+ ETH staked.

## Scalability Strategy
Ethereum prioritizes security and decentralization over raw TPS. Scale via:
- [[layer2-scaling]] — Rollups (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base)
- [[danksharding]] — future blob-carrying transactions
- Proto-danksharding (EIP-4844) deployed 2024

## Ecosystem
- Most DeFi (Aave, Uniswap, Maker)
- Most NFTs (OpenSea, Blur)
- Most stablecoins (USDC primary home)
- DAOs and governance

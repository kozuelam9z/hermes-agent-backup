# Uniswap

## Overview
Uniswap is the dominant decentralized exchange (DEX) built on Ethereum, pioneering the AMM (Automated Market Maker) model. It allows users to swap any ERC-20 token without an order book or centralized intermediary.

## Key Facts
- **Founded:** 2018 by Hayden Adams
- **Inspired by:** Vitalik Buterin's concept of "bonding curves"
- **Version:** V3 (launched May 2021), V4 (draft 2023)
- **TVL:** $5-10B (varies with market)
- **Daily Volume:** $1-5B (largest DEX by volume)
- **Location:** Fully on-chain, no central HQ

## How It Works (V3)
Users provide liquidity to asset pools (e.g., ETH/USDC). In return they receive LP tokens. Traders pay a 0.30% fee on each swap, distributed proportionally to LPs.

**Concentrated liquidity** (V3 innovation): LPs can specify price ranges, using capital more efficiently. A position may only be active within a specific range.

## UNI Token
- Governance token launched Sep 2020
- Airdropped to early users
- Staked for protocol fee revenue (5/6 of fees go to stakers)

## Governance
Uniswap DAO governs the protocol. UNI holders vote on treasury allocations, fee switches, and protocol upgrades. Active governance participation is low (<5% of tokens voting).
